DAEDAL UI Sounds — free sound effects library by DAEDAL

WHAT'S INSIDE
  WAV, 48 kHz / 24-bit, stereo, peak -1 dBFS. File names describe the sound (character, length, direction:
  LR = left to right, RL = right to left). Every sound was synthesized from scratch — no samples, no AI.
  License: CC0 — free for any use, including client work. Credit appreciated, never required.

TIPS
  Whooshes: put the loudest point on the cut; layer with an impact for punch.
  Risers: end exactly on the cut or the beat drop; tension drones sit under suspense scenes at a low level.
  Impacts: "Boom" for titles and drops, "Punch" for fast cuts, "SubDrop" for reveals (needs headphones/subwoofer).
  UI sounds: keep them low (-18 to -12 dB) under music — they should be felt more than heard.
