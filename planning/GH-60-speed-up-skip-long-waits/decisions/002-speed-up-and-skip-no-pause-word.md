# 002: Speed-up and skip; no separate pause word

**Date:** 2026-11-16 (shaping, Q2; the person answered "ok" to the recommendation)

**Question:** what should a viewer see during a long wait: speed-up, skip, a pause screen?

**Decision:** two new effects, **speed-up** (a stretch plays faster, e.g. ×1.5 or ×4, with a small on-screen badge
showing the factor) and **skip** (a stretch is cut out and replaced by a short card such as "⏭ 38 s skipped"). No
separate pause word.

- **Why:** speed-up keeps the motion for medium waits and skip handles very long ones. A pause, a frozen frame while a
  comment speaks, already happens when speech outlasts its action; a word for it would overlap.
- **Open:** the person did not say what else "pause screen" might mean; if it comes back as something other than a
  freeze with a comment, it is a new question.
