# 003: Speech keeps its normal speed and is never cut

**Date:** 2026-11-16 (shaping, Q3; the person answered "yes" to the recommendation)

**Question:** what happens to comments and sounds inside a stretch that is sped up or skipped?

**Decision:**
- A comment is always spoken at normal speed and never cut. In a sped-up stretch it plays while the picture runs
  fast; if it outlasts the stretch, the last frame holds until it ends, as today. A skip never swallows a comment: it
  plays over the "skipped" card.
- Sounds in a sped-up stretch play at their moment, at normal pitch; sounds in a skipped stretch are dropped.

- **Why:** natural narration, transcript and captions stay in sync without changes, and a script can still narrate
  over a wait ("the agent is thinking…"), the main use.
- **Rejected:** speeding speech up with the picture (unnatural, captions drift); forbidding comments in those
  stretches (loses the main use).
