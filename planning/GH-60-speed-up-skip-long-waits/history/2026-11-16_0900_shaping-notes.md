## What exists (looked up 2026-11-16, runtime 0.3.1)

- **Effects** are a third word family beside comments and sounds (GH-27 decision 002): `flash`, `showFrame`,
  `zoomIn`, `zoomOut`; standalone or chained; automatic polish yields to them (decision 003).
- **Timeline:** the renderer already inserts time (`showFrame` frames, speech holds that freeze the last frame), so
  changing the pace of a stretch fits the existing model. Nothing speeds up or cuts real time today.
- **One file, one script, one reel:** a script file's default export is one `script(options, body)`; the result is a
  reel (GH-27 decision 010).
- **Failures:** an action that throws becomes `step N ("comment") failed: …` and aborts the compose
  (`src/composer/steps.ts:63-68`). A plain JS `try/catch` in the body already catches it and the script goes on, but
  the failed attempt stays in the video and nothing in the reel marks it.
- **Related follow-up:** `follow-ups/2026-11-15_1330_zoom-ends-before-the-last-comment.md` (zoom vs. holds between
  scenes); not the same need.

## Decisions

- 001: long waits come first; scenarios per file and failure handling are shaped later.
- 002: speed-up (with a factor badge) and skip (with a "skipped" card); no separate pause word.
- 003: speech keeps normal speed and is never cut; sounds in a skip are dropped.
- 004: one story, two tickets: speed-up (with retiming), then skip + release 0.4.0 + plugin.

## Proposed shape

- **Level:** a story, "Speed up or skip long waits in a reel".
- **Tickets, in order:** (1) speed-up with its factor badge, including the timeline retiming; (2) skip with its
  "skipped" card, release 0.4.0 and the plugin teaching both words.
- **Next:** `/groundwork:create-story`, taking over this folder's decisions 001-004. The words' exact shape in a
  script (wrapping an `await` or marking start and end) is the story's first question.
- **Shaped later, separately:** several scenarios in one script file (changes GH-27 decision 010) and failure
  handling.
