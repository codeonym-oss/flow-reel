# 004: A cassette is the whole compiled result of one script

**Date:** 2026-11-14 (shaping, Q3; agreed: "yes, it the whole compiled result")

**Question:** what is a cassette: the mixed audio, the final video, or everything one compile produces?

**Decision:** **everything one compile of one script produces, as one folder with a manifest:** the video, the
transcript, the captions, the event log and the audio. "Same script, same cassette" is the determinism promise.

- **Why:** it is what the composer compiles and what people share; one name covers the files that must travel
  together.
- **Not chosen:** the mixed audio track only (the full result would have no name); the MP4 only (transcript and
  captions travel separately and can mismatch).
