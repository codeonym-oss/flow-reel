# 002: Visual effects are a third family of script words, beside comments and audio

**Date:** 2026-11-14 (shaping, added by the person after Q1: "there are SFX / visual effects / Effects such as
Flash screen / showFrame(image, duration) ... ZoomIn, ZoomOut etc .... (for the screen recording)")

**Question:** what can a script attach to the recording besides spoken comments and sounds?

**Decision:** **visual effects on the recording**: at least screen flash, `showFrame(image, duration)`, zoom in and
zoom out. Like comments and audio, they are written in the script.

- **Why:** the person wants the script to direct the picture, not only the sound.
- **Open:** how explicit effects relate to today's automatic zoom/cursor/ripple polish (GH-1 decision 012); Q2.
