# 007: The score shows each step's picture, on by default

**Date:** 2026-11-26 (grilling, Q5; agreed: "1")

**Question:** does the score show a small picture of each step above its lines?

**Decision:** **yes: a Pictures line at the top of the score, one small picture per step, taken when the step ran. It is
on by default; "Lines only" hides it.** The choice is remembered in the browser.

- **Why:** a long score is found by eye faster from pictures than from step names; a failed step's picture shows what
  the page looked like.
- **Accepted cost:** one small picture kept per step in the run's folder, and a taller score.
- **Not chosen:** lines only by default (shorter, but the score reads as text only).
