iconic, modern studio, features rich, niche UI")

**Question:** when does the person see and approve pictures of the screens?

in `prototypes/`. Every ticket builds from the approved mockup.** It is made with the brand and design skills, and aims
for an iconic, modern, feature-rich studio of its own, on the score idea (epic decision 024).

  (changes land on finished code).
