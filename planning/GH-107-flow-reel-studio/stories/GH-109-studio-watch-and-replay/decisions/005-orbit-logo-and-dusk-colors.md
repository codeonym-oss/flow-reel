# 005: The logo is the Orbit, in the Dusk colors

**Date:** 2026-11-26 (agreed: "NICE, liked it!" on mark G, then "Dusk" picked for its colors)

**Question:** what is flow-reel's logo, and which colors do the logo and the studio use?

**Decision:** **the logo is mark G, the Orbit: a round player with an empty play shape inside, wrapped twice by a
timeline cut into clips that ends in a round playhead, tilted −10°. Its colors are Dusk: on black, the player is a
yellow-to-emerald gradient and the timeline emerald; on white, a deeper yellow and emerald.** The studio takes the same
colors. This replaces the ivory, ink and red of epic decision 024; the score idea itself stays.

| Token | On black | On white |
| --- | --- | --- |
| background | `#0a0a0a` | `#ffffff` |
| yellow | `#ffd400` | `#e8b500` |
| emerald | `#10b981` | `#059669` |
| alternate background | `#062019` | — |

- **Why:** the person picked yellow, emerald and black, and chose this mark after six rounds of proposals. The orbit
  reads as a player and a timeline, and its playhead can circle as the studio's "running" animation.
- **Accepted cost:** at 16 px the clip gaps and the second turn blur; the favicon needs a simpler, single-turn cut.
- **Not chosen:** marks A to F (see `prototypes/logo-marks.html`), and the Lime, Gold, Jade and Sun color sets.
- **Still to settle:** the favicon cut, and which color marks "now" and what needs a person, when the mockup is redone.
