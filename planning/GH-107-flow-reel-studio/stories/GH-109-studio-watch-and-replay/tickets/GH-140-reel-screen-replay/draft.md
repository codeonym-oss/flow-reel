# Replay a reel in the studio

## Problem

A finished reel is only a video file; jumping to a step or a line means scrubbing blind.

## Change

- **Reel** from the mockup: play, scrub, speed, captions, loop a step; jumping to a step or a transcript line.
- **The score** (ticket 6) under the video, following the playhead.
- The reel's files and its numbers (length, size, loudness).

## Done when

- Clicking a step or a transcript line plays from there.
- The playhead and the score stay together while playing and scrubbing.
