# 003: Play, scrub, speed, captions and loop a step

**Date:** 2026-11-26 (decided alone: the person approved autonomous delivery of story GH-109)

**Decision:**
- **Play / pause** (Space or K), a **track** to scrub (arrows: 5 s; Home, End), **speed** 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2×,
  **captions** from `captions.vtt` on by default, **loop step** (L) repeating the step under the playhead until
  turned off.
- **Transcript:** each step's comment with its time; the current one is marked and kept in view; clicking one plays
  from it.
- The track shows a tick per step; hovering it shows the time.
