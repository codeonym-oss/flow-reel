# The score

## Problem

A run's steps, comments, sounds, effects and cards have no shared timeline to read them on.

## Change

- **The score** from the mockup: one line per kind (pictures, steps, comments, sounds, effects, cards), on a time ruler,
  with the yellow playhead.
- It **writes itself** as the run plays on the Run screen.
- **Step pictures** on by default, "Lines only" to hide them (007); zoom, fit and fold for long scripts.
- Usable by keyboard.

## Done when

- During a run the score grows step by step with the journal.
- A script of 60 steps stays readable, zoomed and folded.
- Every part of the score can be reached and read by keyboard.
