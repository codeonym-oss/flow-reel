# 024: The studio draws the reel as a score

**Date:** 2026-11-25 (grilling; agreed: "ok, lets choose A for now")

**Question:** what is the studio's visual idea?

**Decision:** **for now, a score: each kind of flow-reel word has its own line on a timeline, and the score writes itself as
the run plays.** Ivory or deep ink background, fine lines, precise typography, one red accent for "now" and for what needs
a person. It works in light and dark, and the app being shown stays the brightest thing on screen.

- **Why:** comments, sounds, effects and cards are already separate families of words; it shows rhythm, where a list of
  commands doesn't; a later editor gets a place to work.
- **Accepted cost:** long scripts need zooming and folding, and it takes a minute to learn.
- **Not chosen:** a cutting table (shows timing less well); a projection room (dark tools are common, and lists and settings
  gain little).
- **Open:** whether each step's picture is shown above the lines; everything drawn in detail.
