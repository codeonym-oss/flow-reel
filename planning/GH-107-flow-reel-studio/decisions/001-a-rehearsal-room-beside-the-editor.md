# 001: The web app is a rehearsal room; scripts are still edited elsewhere

**Withdrawn 2026-11-25:** the person says nothing was agreed ("i havent agreed on anything yet! we are starting again"); their "ok" was read as agreement.

**Date:** 2026-11-25 (grilling, Q1; agreed to the recommendation: "ok")

**Question:** what is the web app's main job?

**Decision:** **a rehearsal room.** The person opens a script, rehearses it live (pause, step, replay from the start),
composes it, and scrubs the reel against its cue sheet: the steps with their comments, sounds, effects, cards and
overlays. Editing stays in the person's editor or with their agent; the app follows each save. It ships in this order:
the progress of composes and the replay of reels first, rehearsal after.

- **Why:** it is the only option that covers what was asked: running jobs, step by step, replay, scripts opened by
  path. A compose can't be paused, because the recording runs in real time and its timing is checked, so step by step
  needs a mode of its own that replays the script without recording or voicing it.
- **Not chosen:** a control room with progress and replay only (no live step by step; it is the first part of this
  one); an editing studio that writes back to the script (an epic, and rewriting code people also edit by hand is
  risky; possible later as its own story).
